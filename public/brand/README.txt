Put the original brand artwork in this folder.

logo.png   the full logo lockup, roughly 1200 x 700 pixels with a transparent background
bottle.png any product photos you would like to use later

After adding logo.png, open lib/brand.ts and set:

  logoImage: "/brand/logo.png",

The website then shows the real artwork in the header, the footer and the hero
instead of the emblem that is drawn in code.
